Common questions
What's included in the incorporation fee?
Articles of incorporation, minute book setup, resolutions, share certificates, and all corporate registers — ready to download as PDF.
Do I need to file anything with the government?
Yes — government filing fees are separate and paid directly to the registry. Mortacc prepares the documents; your firm handles the filing.
Can my client sign the engagement letter remotely?
Yes. The engagement letter is a digital form your client completes online — no in-person meeting required.
Is there French support for Québec incorporations?
All documents are available in French. The client intake form and engagement letter are fully bilingual.
