Dear Client,

We are pleased to confirm our engagement to provide corporate incorporation services on your behalf. This letter sets out the terms and conditions of our engagement. Please read it carefully in its entirety before signing.

1. Nature of Services

Mortacc Solutions Inc. ("Mortacc", "we", "us") agrees to provide the following services to you ("Client", "you") in connection with the incorporation of your company:

  • Assessment and recommendation of the appropriate jurisdiction of incorporation;
  • Preparation and submission of the articles of incorporation;
  • Preparation of initial organizational resolutions;
  • Preparation of initial by-laws;
  • Preparation of share certificates for initial shareholders;
  • Digital delivery of all incorporation documents upon completion; and
  • Automated annual return reminders.

These services are administrative in nature. Mortacc Solutions Inc. is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

2. Professional Fees and Disbursements

Our professional fees for corporate services are flat-rate and billed per engagement as follows:

Service Professional Fee What's Included
Corporate — IncorporationC$500Articles, by-laws, resolutions, share certificates
Corporate Pro — Incorporation + Minute BookC$750Full incorporation + complete digital minute book
Minute Book Preparation OnlyC$250Minute book update for existing corporations

Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes (HST/GST/QST). Government filing fees and registry disbursements are billed separately at cost.

3. Client Responsibilities

You agree to provide complete and accurate information, review all draft documents promptly, and notify Mortacc of any errors or changes immediately.

4. Confidentiality

Mortacc agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the information you provide, except where disclosure is required by law or necessary to complete the incorporation.

5. Governing Law

This Engagement Letter is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein.
