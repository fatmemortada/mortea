Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. If you upgrade mid-cycle, we prorate the difference. If you downgrade, changes apply at your next billing cycle.

What counts as a client?

Each corporate client with its own profile counts toward your client limit. Directors, shareholders, appointments, and documents are unlimited on all plans.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes — 14 days, no credit card required. You get a fully functional account pre-loaded with sample data to explore.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored in Canadian data centers (Toronto). We comply with PIPEDA and provincial privacy legislation. See our Security Center for details.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?

Yes — save 17% with annual billing. That is 2 months free compared to monthly.

Can you migrate data from Corporatek, EnAct, or ALF?

Yes. We have structured migration toolkits for major legacy platforms. Enterprise plans include a dedicated migration specialist. Contact us for details.
