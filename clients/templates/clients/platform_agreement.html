2. Description of Services

Mortacc provides a cloud-based entity management and corporate governance platform designed for Canadian accounting and law firms. Services include, but are not limited to:

  • Client onboarding and document collection portals;
  • Corporate incorporation workflows for Federal, Ontario, BC, and Québec jurisdictions;
  • Entity records management, structure charts, and cap table tracking;
  • Compliance deadline monitoring and automated reminders;
  • Billing, invoicing, and document management tools; and
  • Bilingual (EN/FR) platform access.

Mortacc is a technology platform. It does not provide legal, accounting, or tax advice. The Subscriber is solely responsible for ensuring that services rendered to their clients comply with applicable professional standards and regulations.

Access to the platform is subject to payment of the applicable subscription fee as selected at registration. Fees are billed in advance on a monthly or annual basis. All fees are in Canadian dollars and exclusive of applicable taxes (HST/GST/QST). Mortacc reserves the right to update pricing with thirty (30) days' written notice.
